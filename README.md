IS328 Data Mining Flashcards

Interactive revision flashcards for the IS328 final exam. There are 165 cards across 12 topics, each tagged as theory, formula, worked calculation or scenario.

Topics:
· Data pre-processing 
· Decision trees & Random Forest 
· Rule-based classification 
· K-Nearest Neighbour 
· Naïve Bayes 
· Neural networks 
· Linear & logistic regression
· K-Means & hierarchical clustering 
· Association rules (Apriori, lift) 
· Recommender systems 
· Web mining (PageRank, HITS) 
· Privacy-preserving data mining

How to use:
- Click a card or press "Space" to flip; use "← →" to move between cards.
- Mark cards "Got it" or "Review again", then study only the ones you missed.
- Filter to "Calculation" cards for timed practice.
- Use "List view" to print.

Based on Han, Kamber & Pei, "Data Mining: Concepts and Techniques (3rd ed.)". 

P.S These are unofficial study notes; check formulas against your lecture slides.
